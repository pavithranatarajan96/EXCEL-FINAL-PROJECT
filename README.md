# E-commerce Sales Analysis – Excel Final Project

## Project Overview
This project analyzes e-commerce sales data using Microsoft Excel. The goal is to understand sales performance across product categories, stores, and regions, and identify opportunities to improve business performance.

## Objectives
- Clean and prepare the dataset for analysis.
- Merge and organize relevant data from different tables.
- Use Excel formulas to calculate and analyze sales-related metrics.
- Create Pivot Tables to summarize sales performance.
- Compare product categories, stores, and regions.
- Identify high-performing and low-performing categories.
- Recommend areas for further investigation and sales improvement.

## Dataset
**Workbook:** `Ecommerce_Sales_Dataset_updated.xlsx`

The workbook contains e-commerce sales data and related tables. The analysis focuses on available fields such as products, stores, regions, sales, quantities, discounts, and dates.

## Data Preparation
The project includes:
- Reviewing and cleaning the dataset.
- Organizing and merging relevant data.
- Applying Excel formulas to support calculations.
- Preparing the data for Pivot Table analysis.

## Analysis Performed

### 1. Descriptive Analytics
Summarizes what happened in the sales data.
- What is the total revenue?
- Which product category has the highest sales?
- Which region and store generate the highest revenue?

### 2. Diagnostic Analytics
Explores possible reasons behind sales performance.
- What factors may explain differences in sales across categories and regions?
- How do discounts and quantities relate to sales performance?
- What issues may be affecting lower-performing categories?

### 3. Predictive Analytics
Uses historical patterns to explore what may happen next.
- What sales trends can be observed over time?
- What revenue might be expected in a future period?
- Which categories or regions may require closer monitoring?

*Forecasts should be validated using an appropriate forecasting method and historical data.*

### 4. Prescriptive Analytics
Suggests actions based on the analysis.
- How can sales in high-performing categories be improved further?
- What actions may help improve sales in lower-performing categories?
- How can discounts and marketing efforts be targeted more effectively?

## Key Business Focus
Electronics is considered a high-performing category, while Sports is a category for further investigation. The project aims to identify ways to improve Electronics sales and investigate issues affecting Sports sales, with the goal of increasing sales across all regions. These observations should be confirmed against the workbook's actual results.
